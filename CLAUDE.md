# Household Budget

Our household's budget: the statements, bills and pay stubs we receive, what
we decide about money, and a monthly check of spending against `budget.json`.
It is for the two of us — Dana and Sam — and nobody else reads it.

**Follow [`standards/brain-standard.md`](standards/brain-standard.md)** for
everything in `brain/` and `documents/`, and **[`standards/doe.md`](standards/doe.md)**
for every skill and script. Run each standard's checklist when you finish work
it covers.

## What this folder is

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `inbox/` | New paperwork lands here. Empty it with the brain standard. |
| `documents/` | What we received: original PDF + `.json` sidecar. Not in git. |
| `brain/` | What we wrote: decisions, goals, tasks. Its own private repo. |
| `budget.json` | The monthly budget. The only place amounts are set. |
| `.claude/skills/` | Our routines (start with `monthly-summary`). |

## How to work with me

- **Answer from the documents and the brain first**, and name the file you got it from.
- **All money arithmetic is done by a script.** Never add, subtract or total in your head.
- **Explain what you are about to do before you do it**, in plain language.
- **Ask before moving, renaming or deleting** anything outside `inbox/`.
- **Show me a plan first** for anything bigger than one file.
- Plain English, short answers, dollars to the cent.

## Never

- Never invent a number, date or merchant. If it isn't in a document, say so.
- Never commit a statement, pay stub, bill or receipt to git.
- Never put a password, login or full account number in any file.
- Never change an original document. Rename it; don't edit it.
- Never pay, transfer, email or contact anyone.
